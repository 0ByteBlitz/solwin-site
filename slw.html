Who can mint

Only the program, only in settle_spin, only for a losing spin. The mint authority is a program address; there's no freeze authority.

How much

100–1,000 $SLW per 1 SOL bet, skewed small: 45% of mints are 100–300, only 5% reach 800–1,000. Average 397.5 per losing SOL.

The honest caveat

The program is upgradeable, so "no admin mint" is as strong as its upgrade key. Before mainnet that key moves to a multisig, and after an audit the program should be made immutable.

Fully backed before it exists

6% of every bet goes to the buyback reserve. On average a SOL of bets mints $SLW worth 0.0593 SOL at the floor, and the reserve receives 0.06 SOL for it. The token is paid for in SOL at the moment it's mined.

Buyback & burn

Three ways $SLW leaves circulation. The first works today; the others are designed and need a program upgrade, an audit and, for the last one, legal advice.

Floor redemption Live

Sell any amount to the reserve at the floor with buyback. The tokens burn in the same instruction. Works while the game is paused and while you're self-excluded.

When the market dips below the floor, anyone can buy and redeem. That arbitrage pulls the price back up, and it's the first job we offer agents.

Floor ratchet Proposed

From era 1 the reserve grows faster than the tokens it backs. A permissionless ratchet_floor raises the floor by up to 5% a week while backing stays ≥ 125%, never above 0.0002 × 2era, and never down.

Result: the floor can double every halving while RTP stays 95.88%.

Revenue buyback Phase 2

When the market is above the floor nobody redeems. Then 20% of the vault's monthly profit above its solvency target buys $SLW on the market and burns it: by the multisig first, with every transaction published, and on-chain only after an audited DEX integration.

The ratchet rule

circulating  = total_minted − total_burned          // both already on-chain
backing      = reserve ÷ (circulating × floor)
ceiling(era) = 0.0002 SOL × 2^era

ratchet_floor()   // anyone can call it
  require now − last_ratchet ≥ 7 days
  require backing ≥ 125%
  new = min(floor × 1.05, ceiling(era), reserve ÷ (circulating × 1.25))
  require new > floor                               // the floor never goes down
  floor = new

Sinks on the roadmap Proposed

Spin credits: burn $SLW for a bet worth its floor value. Jackpot tickets: burn a fixed amount for one extra jackpot draw, a pure burn that raises backing for every other holder.

What's promised, and what isn't. The code promises the cap, mining only on losses, redemption at the floor, the daily cap and burning on redemption. It promises no market price above the floor and no return. The game has a 4.12% house edge.
