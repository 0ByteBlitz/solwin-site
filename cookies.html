Cookie policy

Cookies and privacy.

Short version: we don't set cookies, we don't track you, and we don't collect personal data. Here's exactly what does happen when you visit.

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Cookies

This site and the SolWin demo set no cookies: no first-party cookies, no third-party cookies, no session cookies. There are no analytics, advertising, social-media or tracking scripts, no pixels, no fingerprinting and no third-party fonts. Every page's Content-Security-Policy only allows scripts and styles from this site, so none can be added by accident.

Because nothing optional is stored or tracked, there's no cookie banner to accept. If that ever changes, we'll ask for your consent first.

Browser storage (the demo only)

The web app at /play/ keeps a few settings and records in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. They never leave your device: nothing reads them except the app in your own browser. The site's other pages store nothing.

KeyWhat it holdsWhy
solwin.sound1 or 0Remembers whether you turned sound effects on or off.
solwin.calm1 or 0Remembers calm mode (no shake, no flashes, fewer particles).
solwin.age-ackYour age tick and the terms version you confirmedRemembers you confirmed you are 18 or over, so you are not asked again. No wallet connects until you tick.
Spin in progress recordA record of any spin in progress, which can identify that spin on the blockchainLets the spin be recovered after a reload. No server copy.
solwin.wallet (walletName in older builds)The name of the wallet you last connected, such as "Phantom"Reconnects the same wallet next time. Never an address, key or balance.

These count as storage you asked for (a setting you chose, or a wallet you connected), so no consent is needed for them. The demo works without them; if your browser blocks storage, it just forgets your choices.

Who your browser talks to

No data is sent to us. Your browser does connect to these services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Solana devnet RPC (api.devnet.solana.com): the live stats on the $SLW page and the demo's devnet panel read public blockchain data from it, so its operator sees your IP address. Requests carry no cookies and no referrer.
  • Your wallet, if you connect one: a browser extension or app you installed, under its own policy. The site never sees your keys or seed phrase.
  • Links you click (Solana Explorer, help organisations, GitHub) take you to other sites with their own policies.

Personal data

  • SolWin has no cookies, no analytics, no backend and no accounts, and collects no personal data itself. Personal data collected by us: none.
  • The site host can see your IP address. The Solana devnet RPC provider can see your IP address, your wallet address and the requests the app makes. We do not receive their logs. They and your wallet provider have their own policies. No wallet connects until you tick the 18+ box.
  • You may have rights over personal data where you live. We hold none we can access or delete, so ask the third parties above about their logs. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law where you live.
  • SolWin is for people 18 or over.

Where it is not available

Not available where prohibited by law.

Do not use SolWin if the law where you live prohibits it.

The blockchain is public

If you send a transaction on devnet (create a profile, set limits, spin, sell $SLW), it's recorded on a public blockchain together with your wallet address. Anyone can read it, it's permanent, and nobody, including us, can delete it. Your player profile account (limits, self-exclusion, totals) is public on-chain in the same way.

How to clear it

  • Turn sound and calm mode back to their defaults in the demo, or disconnect your wallet there.
  • Or delete this site's data in your browser settings (look for "site data" or "cookies and site data" for 0byteblitz.github.io). That removes every key above at once.
  • Private or incognito windows forget everything when you close them.

Changes

If what we store or who your browser talks to changes, this page changes first, and anything optional will need your consent before it's used. Questions about security or privacy: open a private advisory. See also Terms of Use and Privacy Notice.
