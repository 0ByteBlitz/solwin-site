How a spin works

One transaction from your wallet. The randomness comes from MagicBlock VRF, verified on-chain before the program settles your spin.

1

Bet

place_bet splits your SOL: 92% to the vault, 6% to the $SLW reserve, 2% to the jackpot. It asks MagicBlock for randomness in the same transaction.

2

Prove

MagicBlock's oracle answers with a VRF proof. The VRF program checks the proof and calls settle_spin. Nobody, us included, can pick the result.

3

Pay or mine

A win pays SOL straight to your wallet. A loss mints 100–1,000 $SLW per SOL bet. Either way the reels and amounts are on-chain in the SpinSettled event.

Where each bet goes

92% vault6%2%
  • Vault 92%: pays SOL wins. The max bet adjusts automatically to what the vault can cover.
  • Buyback reserve 6%: backs the $SLW floor. Only buyback can take SOL out, and it burns the tokens.
  • Jackpot 2%: 1 in 50,000 per 1 SOL bet wins the whole pool.

Player protection, enforced by the program

Not settings in a web page: rules in the on-chain program that apply to every wallet, human or agent.

Limits that stick

Daily, weekly and monthly loss limits, a wager limit and a max bet. Lowering is instant; raising waits 24 hours.

Cool-off and self-exclusion

From one day to permanent. Selling $SLW keeps working while you're excluded, so you can always cash out.

New wallets start small

0.1 SOL max bet for a wallet's first week, a minimum gap between spins, and a pause switch that can stop new bets but never move funds.
