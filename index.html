How a spin works

One transaction from your wallet. The randomness comes from MagicBlock VRF and is verified on-chain before the program settles your spin.

BET

Your SOL splits three ways

place_bet sends 92% to the vault, 6% to the $SLW reserve and 2% to the jackpot, and asks MagicBlock for randomness in the same transaction.

PROVE

Randomness nobody picks

MagicBlock's oracle answers with a VRF proof. The VRF program checks it and calls settle_spin. Nobody, us included, can choose the result.

PAY / MINE

No spin comes back empty

A win pays SOL straight to your wallet. A loss mints 100–1,000 $SLW per SOL bet. Either way, the reels and amounts are on-chain in SpinSettled.

Where each bet goes

92% VAULT6%2%
  • Vault 92%: pays SOL wins. The max bet adjusts automatically to what the vault can cover.
  • Buyback reserve 6%: backs the $SLW floor. Only buyback can take SOL out, and it burns the tokens.
  • Jackpot 2%: a 1 in 50,000 chance per 1 SOL bet of winning the whole pool.

Player protection, enforced by the program

These aren't settings on a web page. They're rules in the on-chain program and apply to every wallet, human or agent.

LIMITS

Limits that stick

Daily, weekly and monthly loss limits, a wager limit and a max bet. Lowering a limit is instant; raising one waits 24 hours.

PAUSE

Cool-off and self-exclusion

From one day to permanent. Selling $SLW keeps working while you're excluded, so you can always cash out.

NEW

New wallets start small

A 0.1 SOL max bet in a wallet's first week, a minimum gap between spins, and a pause switch that can stop new bets but never move funds.
