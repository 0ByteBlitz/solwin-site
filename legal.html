Terms of Use

What this is

Play money simulation. No real SOL, no prizes, no cash-out. SolWin runs on Solana devnet, a test network. Test SOL and test $SLW have no monetary value and may be reset.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or over; some places require a higher age. SolWin is not available where prohibited by law, so do not use it there. You confirm your age yourself; we do not check it.

Where it is not available

Not available where prohibited by law.

Do not use SolWin if the law where you live prohibits it.

Risks and no advice

Smart contracts, wallets and networks can fail, and test tokens can be lost. You alone control your wallet and keys. Transactions you sign are public and irreversible. Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

Randomness, odds and cancelled spins

Odds and payouts are in How it works. If randomness does not arrive, you can cancel the spin after about 10 minutes. A cancelled spin forfeits the bet.

Fees

Each spin also costs a randomness-service fee of about 0.0005 test SOL plus a small network fee, on top of your bet. These fees go to third parties (the randomness provider and the network), not to SolWin's game vault, and are not refunded, whether the spin wins, loses or is cancelled. A small deposit (about 0.0011 test SOL) is also held for each spin and returned when it settles or is cancelled.

Test tokens and sell-back

Test $SLW can be sold back to the on-chain reserve at a fixed floor, subject to a daily cap. No price, return or market is promised.

Acceptable use

Do not attack, exploit or overload the service, or use it unlawfully. Whoever runs an AI agent is responsible for what it does.

No warranty and liability

Provided as is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses of test tokens or from wallet, network or smart-contract failures. Nothing excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law where you live.

Privacy Notice

Short version

SolWin has no cookies, no analytics, no backend and no accounts, and collects no personal data itself.

Stored in your browser

localStorage holds only your age tick (solwin.age-ack), the last wallet name used, your sound and calm-mode settings, and a record of any spin in progress, so it can be recovered after a reload. These stay on your device; there is no server copy. The spin record can identify that spin on the blockchain. Clear them in your browser settings.

Seen by others

The site host can see your IP address. The Solana devnet RPC provider can see your IP address, your wallet address and the requests the app makes. We do not receive their logs. They and your wallet provider have their own policies. No wallet connects until you tick the 18+ box.

Public blockchain

Wallet addresses and devnet transactions are public. Nobody, including us, can change or delete them. Do not put personal information in transactions.

Your rights

You may have rights over personal data where you live. We hold none we can access or delete, so ask the third parties above about their logs. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law where you live.

Age

SolWin is for people 18 or over.
