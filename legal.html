Risks

  • Gambling risk: you can lose everything you bet. Results are random, and past results say nothing about future ones.
  • Smart contract risk: the program is unaudited and upgradeable. A bug or a bad upgrade could lose or lock funds.
  • Randomness risk: spins depend on an outside oracle. If it fails, a spin may not settle, and cancelling a stuck spin forfeits the bet.
  • Wallet and key risk: anyone with your seed phrase controls your wallet. SolWin will never ask for it.
  • Regulatory risk: laws on gambling and crypto-assets differ by country and change. A change could stop SolWin, or make your use of it unlawful.
  • Network risk: devnet is a test network with no guarantees of uptime or permanence.
